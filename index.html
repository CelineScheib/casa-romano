01 / THE ESSENCE

THE ART OF LIVING

Some places are
simply felt.

There are places whose character cannot be measured in square metres alone. Places where light, landscape and stillness create an atmosphere all their own.

Elevated above the coastline in the private urbanisation of La Alqueria, Casa Romano brings together traditional Mediterranean architecture, luminous living spaces, mature gardens and open horizons extending towards the sea.

From the first morning light to the distant lights of the coast at night, the residence invites a quieter, more considered rhythm of life.

02 / THE RESIDENCEAN ANDALUSIAN SENSE OF PLACE

An architecture of
light and belonging.

Built in the spirit of an Andalusian finca, the residence unfolds across several levels. Solid masonry, sheltered outdoor spaces and windows opening in different directions shape a home that responds naturally to the Mediterranean climate.

I

Natural light

Generous windows bring daylight into the living rooms and corridors while architectural shade provides comfort in summer.

II

Indoor & outdoor

Five terrace connections on the ground level and two additional upper-level accesses link the home to its surroundings.

III

Enduring materials

Warm Mediterranean tile floors, a bespoke solid-granite staircase and a principal bathroom clad in travertine.

03 / OUTDOOR LIVING

Life flows
outside.

The generous east- and south-facing covered terrace is one of the defining spaces of Casa Romano.

Morning sunshine, a lofty shaded roof and room for long lunches or memorable gatherings make it a natural extension of the interiors. As the day unfolds, the poolside alternates between sunlight and shade.

06 / THE LOCATION

Above the coast.
Close to nature.

Casa Romano is located in La Alqueria, a private owners' urbanisation in the hills of Mijas, at approximately 400 metres above sea level.

Approached from Avenida Real via a private driveway, the property enjoys a secluded setting with expansive views across the Andalucian landscape to the Mediterranean.
